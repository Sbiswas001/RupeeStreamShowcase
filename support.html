RupeeStream Support

Find straightforward answers to common questions about backup, recovery, and security. Need personalized help? Contact us directly.

Google Drive Backup

RupeeStream allows you to create optional encrypted backups directly to your Google Drive account.

How it works: Your database is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before upload. The file is stored strictly in your private Google Drive appDataFolder.

RupeeStream cannot access, modify, or read your normal personal files or documents in Google Drive.

Restoring Your Data

To restore your financial records on a new or reset Android device:

  1. Sign in with the same Google Account used during the backup.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Backup & Restore in RupeeStream.
  3. Select Restore from Google Drive.

The encrypted backup is downloaded and decrypted locally on your device using your encryption credentials.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is strictly utilized to authenticate access to your Google Drive appDataFolder and to support encrypted backup-key recovery via Google Play Blockstore.

RupeeStream does not use Google Sign-In to create a remote RupeeStream account or profile, and no account details are transmitted to any RupeeStream server.

RupeeStream+ (₹99)

RupeeStream+ is a one-time lifetime purchase priced at ₹99 that unlocks extended personalization features and theme customization.

Restoring your purchase: Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. If you reinstall or switch devices, your entitlement is automatically recognized when signed into the same Google Play account.

App Lock & Biometrics

RupeeStream leverages the Android Keystore and Android's native BiometricPrompt API to secure access via your fingerprint, face unlock, or app PIN.

Biometric credentials never leave your device's hardware security module and are never accessible to RupeeStream.

General Troubleshooting

Missing Backup: Verify that you have an active internet connection and that you are signed into the correct Google account in Android settings.

Notifications Not Showing: Ensure that system notification permissions are granted in Android Settings for RupeeStream to receive local bill and budget reminders.

Data Clearance: Clearing app data in Android system settings will delete your local records. Ensure your Google Drive backup is up to date before clearing storage.
