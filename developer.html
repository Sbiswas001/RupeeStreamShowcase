Flagship Android Project · Aug 2026 – Present Production Architecture

RupeeStream — Local-First Personal Finance Platform

RupeeStream is a modern personal finance application built natively for Android using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3, designed for comprehensive personal financial management with total user privacy and zero external tracking.

  • Comprehensive Finance System: Multi-account management, transactions, budgets, savings goals, recurring payments, subscriptions, analytics, and actionable financial insights.
  • Explainable Financial Health Score: Engineered a 4-pillar financial health model covering Spending Control, Cash Flow, Savings, and Commitment Load to transform raw data into actionable clarity.
  • Advanced Android Architecture: Room database with migrations, Hilt dependency injection, Coroutines/Flow, WorkManager background jobs, and Jetpack Glance home-screen widgets.
  • Privacy & Cryptography: Biometric/PIN App Lock, encrypted local storage, Android Keystore integration, AES-GCM + PBKDF2 encryption, and client-side encrypted Google Drive backup/restore.
  • On-Device Intelligence: Safe to Spend calculations, spending analysis, predictive runway forecasting, and merchant normalization computed on-device.
