Story & Philosophy

About RupeeStream

Privacy-focused personal finance management for Android.

The Local-First Philosophy

In an era where modern fintech apps routinely centralize sensitive financial lives into remote databases, trade personal data with advertisers, and build user profiles, RupeeStream was created to prove that money management should be entirely personal, secure, and private.

RupeeStream is built from the ground up around a strict local-first architecture:

"Your finances. Your device. Your control."
All account balances, transactions, budgets, insights, and categorizations are computed and stored directly on your phone.

Core Principles

  • Zero Surveillance: No advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, no advertising identifiers, and no telemetry tracking your money habits.
  • On-Device Intelligence: Intelligent assistance, Safe-to-Spend calculations, and transaction predictions run on your phone without sending your financial data to external AI servers.
  • Encrypted Optional Backups: When you choose to backup your data, it is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before uploading directly to your private Google Drive appDataFolder. RupeeStream has no backend server in between.
  • Respectful Pricing: No recurring monthly subscriptions or lock-in. Essential features are free, and optional personalization is accessible via a one-time ₹99 lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing.
