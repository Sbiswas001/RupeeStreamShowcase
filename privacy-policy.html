1. Introduction

RupeeStream is a privacy-focused personal finance application developed by Sayan Biswas. RupeeStream is designed as a local-first application. Your financial information is stored and processed on your device. RupeeStream does not operate a server that stores your financial records.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

RupeeStream may store information that you enter into the application, including:

  • Transactions and transaction descriptions
  • Income and expenses
  • Accounts and balances
  • Budgets
  • Categories
  • Recurring payments and bills
  • Financial goals and contributions
  • Payment instrument information such as card last-four digits and UPI app information
  • Transaction attachments
  • Financial insights, predictions, and financial health calculations
  • Application preferences and settings

This information is stored locally using the application's database and Android application storage.

3. Google Drive Backup

RupeeStream optionally provides Google Drive backup and restore. When enabled, RupeeStream requests access to its private Google Drive appDataFolder. Your database is encrypted on your device before upload using AES-256-GCM. The backup is stored in your private application-data area. RupeeStream cannot access your other Google Drive files and does not operate a server that stores your backup.

You can delete your RupeeStream Google Drive backup through the backup controls provided within the application.

4. Google Account Information

Google Sign-In is used only to authorize Google Drive backup and restore and to support backup-key recovery. RupeeStream may receive your Google account identifier and email address for this purpose. RupeeStream does not use your Google account to create a separate RupeeStream account and does not transmit your Google account information to a RupeeStream server.

5. Google Play Billing

RupeeStream offers an optional RupeeStream+ purchase through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. RupeeStream does not receive or store your credit card number, bank account information, billing address, or other payment credentials. RupeeStream stores the local entitlement status required to determine whether RupeeStream+ features are unlocked.

6. On-Device Processing

RupeeStream performs its financial calculations and intelligent features locally on your device. Transaction predictions, transaction categorization assistance, financial health calculations, and financial advice are generated using local processing. Your financial information is not sent to an external AI service for these features.

7. Data Sharing

RupeeStream does not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics companies. When you explicitly use Google services such as Google Drive backup, the information required to provide that feature is transmitted directly to the applicable Google service.

8. Advertising and Tracking

RupeeStream does not contain advertising SDKs or advertising networks and does not use advertising identifiers for tracking or profiling.

9. App Security

RupeeStream uses Android security mechanisms including Android Keystore, AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-based protection for PIN material, Android BiometricPrompt, encrypted Google Drive backups, and HTTPS/TLS for communication with Google services.

Biometric information such as fingerprint or face templates is handled by Android and is not accessible to RupeeStream.

10. Notifications

RupeeStream may request notification permission for local bill and recurring-payment reminders, budget alerts, and goal milestone notifications. These notifications are generated by the application and do not require a remote push-notification service.

11. Data Retention

Your financial information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall RupeeStream. If enabled, encrypted Google Drive backups remain in your Google Drive application-data storage until you delete them. RupeeStream does not maintain a server-side database containing your financial information.

12. Data Deletion

You can delete financial information through the application's available deletion controls. Clearing RupeeStream's application data or uninstalling the application removes locally stored application data from the device, subject to Android's storage and security mechanisms. Google Drive backups are separate from local application data and remain in Google Drive until deleted. You can delete a RupeeStream Google Drive backup using the backup deletion functionality provided in the application.

13. Children's Privacy

RupeeStream is a personal finance application and is not specifically directed toward children. RupeeStream does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a RupeeStream server.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RupeeStream's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the public privacy-policy URL associated with the application.
