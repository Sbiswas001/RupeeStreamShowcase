Privacy Principles

Local-first by design.

RupeeStream does not operate a remote server that stores your financial records. Everything you track stays in your hands.

✓

100% On-Device Financial Storage

Transactions, account balances, budgets, and goals are stored in your device's private SQLite database.

✓

Encrypted Google Drive Backups

Optional backups are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM before upload, stored solely in your private appDataFolder.

✓

Zero Advertising & Zero Trackers

No advertising SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party profiling, and no data brokers.

✓

On-Device Intelligence

Transaction predictions, categorization assistance, and financial health scores run locally without transmitting data to remote AI APIs.

Capabilities

Crafted for mindful money management.

All the powerful tools you need to budget, track, and understand your finances without trading away your privacy.

Transaction Tracking

Record income and expenses seamlessly with rich descriptions, categories, payment instruments, and transaction attachments.

Accounts Management

Manage bank accounts, cash wallets, credit cards, and UPI apps in one unified balance overview.

Smart Budgets

Set flexible monthly spending limits across categories to stay disciplined before you overspend.

Recurring Payments & Bills

Stay on top of recurring subscriptions, utility bills, and EMI schedules with on-device local notifications.

Financial Goals

Define savings targets, track milestone contributions, and monitor your progress toward life goals.

Financial Insights

Understand spending habits and cash flow trends through intuitive visual breakdowns computed on your phone.

Safe-to-Spend

Instant dynamic calculation of how much discretionary money you can safely spend today without falling short on bills.

Financial Health Assessment

Get an on-device score evaluating savings consistency, debt ratios, and budget adherence with actionable tips.

Local Categorization Assistance

Intelligent transaction predictions and categorization assistance computed locally on-device without remote AI APIs.

Google Drive Backup

Optional encrypted backup to your private Google Drive appDataFolder with AES-256-GCM and key recovery.

App Lock & Biometrics

Protect your financial records with Android BiometricPrompt, PIN authentication, and shoulder-surfing privacy mode.

Home-Screen Widgets

Glance at today's Safe-to-Spend balance and log transactions quickly right from your Android home screen.
