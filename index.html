Privacy Principles

Local-first by design.

RupeeStream does not operate a remote server that stores your financial records. Everything you track stays strictly on your device.

  • 100% On-Device SQLite Storage

    Transactions, account balances, budgets, and goals are stored locally in your device's sandboxed SQLite database.

  • AES-256 Google Drive & Offline JSON Export

    Encrypted backups stay exclusively in your private Google Drive appDataFolder, or export raw JSON files locally without any Google account.

  • Recent Apps Protection (FLAG_SECURE)

    Configured with Android's system-level FLAG_SECURE to prevent screenshots and hide balance previews in the recent apps switcher.

  • Zero Advertising & Zero Trackers

    No advertising SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party profiling, and no data brokers. Works completely offline.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RupeeStream's local-first architecture, encrypted backups, and pricing.

Is my financial data uploaded to any remote servers?

No. Never. RupeeStream operates on a strict local-first architecture. All accounts, transactions, budgets, recurring bills, and savings goals are stored solely inside your Android device's private, sandboxed SQLite database.

RupeeStream does not maintain any backend database, zero advertising SDKs, and zero cloud analytics trackers. Your data remains 100% in your hands.

How do encrypted backups and local exports work?

RupeeStream provides two flexible backup mechanisms:

1. Encrypted Google Drive Backup: Backups are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before upload and stored strictly within your private Google Drive appDataFolder. Encryption keys are protected by the hardware-backed Android Keystore and Google Play Blockstore. RupeeStream has zero access to your other Google Drive files.

2. Offline Local File Export: If you avoid cloud storage, export a raw JSON/database backup directly to your device storage or SD card with zero Google account connection required.

• Dynamic Color (Material You): Theme harmonizes dynamically with your Android wallpaper.
• Dashboard Customization: Add, remove, and reorder financial cards on your dashboard.
• Custom App Launcher Icons: Choose from Emerald Mint, Midnight Gold, Violet, and Obsidian.

Does RupeeStream support UPI apps and Indian bank accounts?

Yes! RupeeStream is tailored for Indian users while fully supporting international multi-currency workflows. You can configure bank accounts, cash wallets, credit cards, and UPI payment instruments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED).

You can also configure custom currency symbols, including INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and more.

What is "Safe-to-Spend" and how is it calculated?

Safe-to-Spend is a dynamic daily calculation computed 100% on-device. It calculates how much discretionary money you can safely spend today without falling short on bills.

It takes your net income, deducts fixed recurring subscriptions, upcoming utility bills, and allocated budget targets, then divides the remaining discretionary pool by the days left in the month.

How do I delete my data or contact developer support?

Because all data is stored on-device, you can completely erase your records anytime by clearing app data in Android settings or using the in-app deletion button. Google Drive backups can also be deleted directly from RupeeStream settings.
